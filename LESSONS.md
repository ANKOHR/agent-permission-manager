# Lessons for KOML

Explicit risk signals can drive reproducible permission reductions, but a policy score is not a calibrated probability. Fixture tests validate the assumption that counts and decisions can be computed exactly; they do not validate that these weights describe real threats. Real usefulness remains unmeasured.

An operating rule for future builds: separate declared observations, deterministic policy decisions, and runtime enforcement. Test boundaries and corrupt the inputs to prove the outputs are computed. Do not claim a security guarantee from synthetic accuracy.
